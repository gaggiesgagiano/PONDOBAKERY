Help

Quick guide

What to do, and when. Everything is in US$; sales, costs and stock values are excluding VAT.
Open the staff manual
Every day – after closing

💵 Sales & money

  1. Daily sales: Sales & Cash-Up → Daily sales – type the sales per category from the till's end-of-day report (excl. VAT). Day and night shift together.
  2. Cash-up: count the drawer and type what the till says per payment type. The program shows if the till is over or short. A manager signs it off.
  3. Waste: Waste – one log per shift for everything thrown away, damaged, given to staff or returned.
  4. Dashboard: nothing to type. It shows today, the week and the month against break-even and your profit target. Press Print to send it to the directors.
Every day – before each shift

🥖 Production

  1. Production → start the plan for the day or night shift. Products with a model stock fill in by themselves.
  2. Type what is still on the shelf; the program works out what to make, the batches and the ingredients.
  3. Print the production sheet, the ingredient pull list and the recipe cards for the bakers. Mark the plan Done afterwards – that takes the ingredients off the stock.
When goods come in from suppliers

📦 Stock

  1. Goods received (GRV): type what really arrived and the invoice prices. Leave "update the ingredient prices" ticked to keep your costing right.
  2. Order from the stock levels page – "Order what's low" fills in the order for you.
Every week – stock take day (e.g. Sunday night)

📋 Stock take

  1. Stock take → start, print the count sheet, count everything in the unit shown (kg, litre, each).
  2. Type the counts and make it final – the stock value is saved.
  3. Reports → Stock results shows the real GP between two stock takes, and the "unexplained loss".
Month end

📊 Reports, payroll & equipment

  1. Payroll: Staff & Payroll → Payroll – start the pay run, type days, overtime and advances, make it final and print the payslips.
  2. Equipment count: Equipment → Monthly count – walk around, count, save. Missing items turn red.
  3. Month-end report: Reports – profit & loss, cash, VAT for the accountant. Check GP against the 60% target.
  4. Lock the month on the Month-end report, so the figures go into the Month archive and don't change any more.
  5. Plug in the flash drive and click Back up now.

🎨 Look, brand & signs

  1. My look (bottom of the menu): pick your own colours – Pondo Classic, Orange, Teal, Warm Orange, Bakery Cream or the dark Late Shift – the heading font and a bigger text size. Only you see it.

  3. Our Brand: mission, values, the brand colours with their codes, fonts, and every logo to download for WhatsApp, signs and printing.
  4. Price cards & posters: shelf cards, display cards, “Today's special” posters and a price list in the brand colours.

When a supplier price changes

  1. Change it on the ingredient (or let the GRV update it). Every recipe cost updates by itself.
  2. Check Recipes & Prices for GP turning warm orange or burgundy, and change the selling price.

Adding a new product

  1. Recipes → New recipe (or Copy a similar one).
  2. Pick ingredients from the list, type the quantities, method, baking temperature and selling price.
  3. Add a photo on the recipe page.

Golden rules

  1. Sales, costs and stock values are excl. VAT.
  2. An ingredient without a price counts as $0 – fix it straight away.
  3. Back up to the flash drive every week.
  4. Give each person their own login, with only the rights they need (Settings → Users & rights).

Something looks wrong?

  • GP is red or below 0: the selling price is missing or too low, or a quantity is wrong (e.g. 5 instead of 0.005).
  • The dashboard shows $0: no daily sales typed for that day.
  • Stock levels look wrong: do a stock take; make sure production plans are marked Done.
  • The program does not open: double-click "Start Pondo.bat" in the Pondo Bakery folder and send Claude a photo of the black window.

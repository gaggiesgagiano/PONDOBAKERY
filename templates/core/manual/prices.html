Selling prices are typed straight into Recipes & Prices – one page with every product, its cost, its selling price and its GP. There is no separate price page.

Change selling prices managers

  1. Recipes & Prices (or the Change prices tile in the Short Cuts). The selling prices are the teal boxes.
  2. Click a price and type the new one (incl. VAT). Press Tab to go to the next product. A changed price turns orange, and the GP % changes while you type.
  3. Save prices in the bar at the bottom. Saved lines show “✓ saved”. Every change is written in the change history.

The GP colours: teal = on target (60% and up), orange = below target, solid red = below 35%. The column Price for 60% shows the price that would give the target GP.

GP check people who may see costs

  1. GP check in the menu (under Bakery & Stock) opens the products with a GP under 50%.
  2. Or type your own range in the orange GP check box – from … % to … % → Show – or click Under 35%, Under 50%, 50 – 60% or 60% and up. Pick a category to narrow it down.
  3. Change the prices right there and Save prices. Show everything takes you back to the full list.

Price cards & posters everybody with access to prices

  1. Recipes & Prices → Price cards & posters.
  2. Choose what to print: shelf cards (8 per A4 page), big cards (4 per page), posters (“Today's special”, 1 per page) or a price list.
  3. Choose the colours: one brand colour, or Mix – the brand checkerboard.
  4. Keep “show the product photo” ticked: products with a photo print with it, the others with a bakery drawing. Tick the “ONLY $…” sticker if you want it, and type the poster headline.
  5. Tick the products (use the search box) → Open to print → Print / save as PDF.
Cut the shelf cards on the white lines between them. Put up new cards the same day a price changes.

Orders (its own item in the menu) is where you order ingredients and packaging from suppliers, send the order by e-mail or WhatsApp, and see when it was sent, how and by whom. Order numbers go PO-1001, PO-1002 …

Make an order

  1. Orders → + New order – or on Stock levels press Order what's low from this supplier.
  2. Choose the supplier, the order date and the delivery date.
  3. Under Items: pick each ingredient, type the number of packs (e.g. 4 bags of 25 kg) and the pack price. Add notes or delivery instructions (“Deliver before 7 am”).
  4. Save order. The order is now a Draft.

Send it to the supplier – two ways

Open the order. At the top are Print order, ✉ E-mail to supplier and the green WhatsApp order button.

1 · By e-mail

  1. Press ✉ E-mail to supplier. The supplier's e-mail address, a subject (“Order PO-1001 – Pondo Bakery”) and a short message are filled in. The order is attached as a PDF.
  2. Check the address – several addresses are separated with commas – and change the message if you want. CC sends a copy to somebody else.
  3. Leave Mark the order as sent to supplier ticked → Send e-mail. It takes a few seconds to make the PDF. A copy goes to the Pondo inbox.
E-mail only works once the Manager has set it up under Settings › E-mail. Without it the Send button stays grey.

2 · By WhatsApp

  1. Press WhatsApp order.
  2. Country: Zimbabwe +263 or South Africa +27. It is used when the number starts with 0. A number typed with +27 or +263 in front always keeps its own country.
  3. Supplier's WhatsApp number – filled in from the supplier. Under it Pondo shows Goes to: +27 82 123 4567 (South Africa) – check it is right.
  4. Message – the whole order is typed out for you: “Good day …”, the order number, our account number, the delivery date, every item with packs, pack size and code, your notes and your name. Change anything you like; words between *stars* show in bold on WhatsApp.
  5. If the supplier has no number yet, leave Save this number on the supplier for next time ticked. Leave Mark the order as sent to supplier ticked.
  6. Press Open in WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens in a new tab (or the WhatsApp app) with the message ready – press send there. Pondo goes back to the order.
The WhatsApp message is text only – no PDF. Need the PDF too? Use Print order → Save as PDF and attach it in WhatsApp, or send it by e-mail as well.

Sent to supplier – the record

Every order page has a Sent to supplier list: the date, the time, how (✉ E-mail or WhatsApp), the e-mail address or number it went to, who sent it and the details (the e-mail subject). An e-mail that could not be sent is listed too, marked failed with the reason – fix it and send again.

A WhatsApp is written down the moment you press Open in WhatsApp – Pondo can't see inside WhatsApp, so make sure you really press send there.

After sending

  • Sent it another way (phone call, in person)? Press Mark as sent.
  • Need to change it? Edit (while it is Draft or Sent) – or in the order list press Edit and click the order. Tell the supplier about the change.
  • When the goods arrive: open the order → Receive this order. The GRV starts with everything that was ordered – see the next chapter.
  • Not needed any more: Cancel this order at the bottom. Cancelled orders stay in the list.
  • The Orders on the way Short Cut (switch it on under Choose short cuts) shows how many sent orders have not arrived yet.

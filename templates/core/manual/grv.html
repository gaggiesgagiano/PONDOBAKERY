A GRV (goods received voucher) records what really arrived from a supplier, at the prices on their invoice. It adds the goods to the stock and keeps the ingredient prices – and so every recipe cost – up to date. GRV – goods received has its own item in the menu.

Receive goods every time goods come in

  1. Ordered from Pondo? Open the order → Receive this order – the items and packs ordered are filled in. Not ordered? GRV – goods received → + Receive goods (or the Receiving Short Cut).
  2. Choose the supplier and the date received, and type the supplier's invoice number and the invoice total.
  3. Under What arrived, for every item: the number of packs that really arrived (count them!) and the pack price on the invoice. Items that did not come: type 0.
  4. Write problems in Notes / problems – short delivered, damaged, wrong item, expired.
  5. Leave Update the ingredient prices to these invoice prices ticked → Save – add to stock.
Before you save, check that the total on the screen matches the invoice total. If not, look for a typing mistake or an item missing from the invoice.

After saving

  • The goods are added to the stock levels and the order is marked Received.
  • New prices go into the ingredients and the price history. Then check GP check for products whose GP dropped.
  • Print the GRV and staple it to the supplier's invoice for the office.
  • Mistake? Open the GRV → Edit – or in the list press Edit and click the GRV.
Stock bought from suppliers goes on a GRV – never on Expenses. The GRVs of the month make up Purchases by supplier in the month-end report.

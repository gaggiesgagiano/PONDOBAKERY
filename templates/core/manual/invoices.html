Quotations, pro-forma invoices and tax invoices for customers who order in bulk – printed on the Pondo letterhead. Numbers go QT-1001, PF-1001 and INV-1001.

Make a quotation or invoice

  1. Invoices & Quotes (menu, Every day) → + Quotation, + Pro-forma or + Invoice.
  2. Pick the customer – or type a new one and tick Add to my customers.
  3. Date, due date / valid until (empty = 14 days later), the customer's order number and who prepared it.
  4. Add the products: pick one and the selling price fills in by itself (incl. VAT). You can also type anything, like “Delivery”, with a price. Type the quantity and a discount % if any.
  5. Save → Print / PDF to print or send it.

Next steps

  • Quotation accepted? Press Customer accepted, then Make pro-forma or Make tax invoice – the lines are copied.
  • Going out with a driver? Send with a driver (delivery) – see the chapter Deliveries with a driver.
  • ✉ E-mail sends the invoice or quotation as a PDF to the customer (check the address and the message first).
  • When the money comes in: Mark as paid with the date and how it was paid.
  • Wrong? Edit, or Cancel this document – cancelled numbers stay in the list.

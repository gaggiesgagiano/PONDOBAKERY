This chapter is for you as the owner of ProBa / Pondo – how the whole program fits together, what to do every day, week, month and year, what only you can do, and what to do when something goes wrong. The other chapters explain each screen step by step.

How Pondo works

  • One program, one computer. Pondo runs on the main laptop – no internet and no outside company is needed. All the figures are in one file in the Pondo folder (data › pondo.sqlite3). Other computers and phones on the shop Wi-Fi open it in Chrome (Settings, users & backups chapter).
  • Start it every morning with the Pondo icon and keep the laptop awake, plugged in and on the inverter. When the laptop sleeps, nobody can work – the till included.
  • After an update, restart Pondo (close the small Pondo window, start it again). New screens only show after a restart; changes to the database are made by themselves when it starts.
  • Backups: Pondo copies itself on the laptop every time it starts, and after each trading day the flash drive backup is required before work can carry on. Keep a second flash drive at home and swap them every week.

How the figures flow – type once, use everywhere

  1. Ingredients (pack size, price, VAT, supplier) → Recipes (what goes in, yield) → the cost of every product and its GP.
  2. Selling prices (Products & Prices) → the till, price cards and invoices.
  3. Orders → the supplier delivers → GRV: the quantities go into stock, the prices update the ingredients, and the GRV becomes an invoice we owe the supplier (Money › Suppliers we owe).
  4. Till sales take stock out (through the recipes), make the cash-up at the end of each shift, the daily sales and the product sales by themselves.
  5. The Saturday stock take compares what is really there with what Pondo expected → variance report → recount → a manager finalises → stock is corrected.
  6. Expenses, payroll and equipment (depreciation) → the Month-end report: profit and loss against your target.
  7. Everything that moves money → Money & accounts: the cash book (safe, bank, EcoCash), suppliers owed, VAT, the balance sheet → the Accountant's month pack.

Your routine

  • Every day: look at the dashboard (sales against target, GP, over / short). Check that every till is closed and every cash-up signed off. Cash drops and pay-outs make sense. Waste logged. Flash drive backup done after the last cash-up.
  • Every week: Saturday stock take – print the empty count sheets, count, look at the variance report, recount the big ones, finalise (managers only; reasons for every variance of $100 or more). Look at Over & short per cashier. Pay the suppliers that are due (Money › Suppliers we owe). Swap the flash drives.
  • Every month: pay run (finalise, print payslips), pay PAYE, NSSA and ZIMDEF and type the payments, equipment count, check the bank statement and count the safe, compare supplier statements, Month-end report, Accountant's month pack to the accountant, then lock the month (Reports and Money chapters).
  • Every year: check the PAYE tax table and NSSA rates with ZIMRA / NSSA (Settings › Tax & NSSA rates), review selling prices and GP, review the SOPs and let staff re-sign them, renew the staff cards that look worn, re-write the SOP exams for everybody.

What only you (the Manager login) can do

  • Users & rights: add people, choose their role and ticks, reset passwords, switch off people who left. Give See costs & profit, Make flash drive backups, Letters and Money & accounts only to people who really need them.
  • Staff cards: Settings › Staff cards – a photo card with a barcode for every staff member. Choose each person's Pondo login next to their name so the card logs them in, and lets Supervisors and managers approve refunds, pay-outs, cash drops and no-sales by scanning. Make missing cards & print all; a lost card → New card (the old one stops at once). When somebody leaves, their card stops by itself.
  • SOP exams: Help & settings › SOP exams – five different papers of 100 questions with memos. You must type your password again to open them, and they lock again after 10 minutes. Never leave them printed where staff can see them.
  • Settings: company, shop details, VAT, targets, look & feel, e-mail, tax rates; Money › Accounts set-up; deleting a supplier payment.
  • Letters with your signature – your signature is only used by your login.

Before opening day

  1. All practice entries are cleared (sales, till slips, cash-ups, waste, GRVs, orders, stock takes) – ingredients, recipes, prices, suppliers, staff and settings stay. This is a once-off job done with your developer's help.
  2. The daily backup lock is switched on from the opening day.
  3. Opening stock take – count everything and finalise it on the day before opening.
  4. Money › Accounts set-up: Safe, Bank and EcoCash with the opening day and the real balances. Suppliers' payment terms typed.
  5. Staff records complete with photos → staff cards printed, laminated and handed out; everybody's login and password tested.
  6. Tills: float in the safe, receipt printer tested, every cashier practised a sale, a refund, a cash drop and closing the till.
  7. Every staff member read and signed the SOPs and wrote an SOP exam.

Keeping it safe

  • Every person has their own login – never share passwords or staff cards. Change your own password now and then; Pondo blocks a name for 15 minutes after 5 wrong passwords.
  • Look at the login register now and then (Settings › Login register) for strange log-ins or many wrong passwords.
  • Recipes belong to the company (SOP 3.06) – bakers see recipes without costs, and nobody may photograph or copy them.
  • The flash drive backups hold everything, salaries included – keep them locked away.
  • The brand font (Retro Star) is licensed for use in this program only – don't copy it for other uses.

When something goes wrong

  • Pondo doesn't open on another computer or phone: is Pondo running on the main laptop, is the laptop awake, is everybody on the same Wi-Fi? Try the number address. Still not: run Allow phones and other computers in the Pondo folder.
  • A new screen or change doesn't show: restart Pondo.
  • "Backup needed" screen: put the flash drive in and press Back up now.
  • Somebody forgot their password: Settings › Users & rights → type a new one → Set. They can also scan their staff card.
  • The receipt doesn't print: check the printer is the default printer and switched on; the slip can be reprinted under Sales / refunds.
  • Stock is wrong / negative: a GRV not typed, a recipe with the wrong quantities, or waste not logged. The stock take puts it right.
  • The bank doesn't agree with Pondo: Money chapter, step 4.
  • The laptop is broken or stolen: a new laptop, copy the Pondo folder from the latest flash drive backup, start Pondo – everything up to that backup is back.

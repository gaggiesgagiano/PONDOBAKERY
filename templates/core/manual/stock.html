Stock keeps track of what is in the store: the stock levels, the weekly stock take and the suppliers. Ordering from suppliers is in its own chapter, Orders, and goods coming in in GRV – goods received. The tabs on these pages are Stock levels · Orders · Receiving (GRV) · Stock take · Suppliers.

Stock levels

  • What is in stock for every ingredient, worked out from the last stock take + goods received (GRVs) − what was used: every product sold on the till and every product logged as waste takes its ingredients out (from the recipes, mother recipes included; refunds put them back). A manager can switch this to production plans marked Done on the Stock take page.
  • Items below their re-order level are marked low. Set the levels on the ingredient (Re-order when stock is below and Order up to).
  • Pick a supplier to see only their items, or tick Only low stock. Order what's low from this supplier makes a new order with the right packs filled in.
  • New order and Receive goods at the top are quick ways into Orders and GRV. Excel downloads the list.

Stock take every Saturday 14:00 – 16:00 (SOP 5.05)

  1. Stock take → Start stock take. Pondo notes the time – expected stock is worked out to that moment.
  2. Print count sheets (empty): the sheets show the items and units only, never what the stock should be – so the count is honest.
  3. The Supervisor and a baker count (one counts, one writes), then type the counts in Pondo → Save & see the variance report.
  4. Variance report: every item with expected, counted, the difference and its value – short in red, over in green, the biggest first, with totals short / over / net.
  5. Recount the differences shows only the items that are out (or not counted yet). Recount them, save, and look at the report again – as often as needed until you are satisfied.
  6. Every difference of $100 or more (managers can change the amount) needs a written reason – e.g. bag torn, GRV typed wrong, product not logged as waste. Save reasons.
  7. Only a manager (Manager or Assistant Manager) can press Finalise stock take. Pondo refuses while items are not counted (type 0 if there are none) or big differences have no reason. From then on stock starts from these counts.

The final variance report (with the reasons) stays in Pondo and can be printed for the file. Reports → Stock results shows the real GP. The Stock take Short Cut says “Due” when the last one is a week old.

Suppliers

  1. Suppliers tab → + New supplier, or click a supplier to change it.
  2. Type the short name, the company name, the contact person, the cell / phone (the WhatsApp number), the e-mail, the address and our account number with them → Save.
Fill in the e-mail and cell number of every supplier – orders are sent to them by e-mail or WhatsApp straight from Pondo. Type South African numbers with +27 in front (e.g. +27 82 123 4567) and Zimbabwean numbers as 07… or +263…. The contact person's name is used in “Good day …” at the top of every order.

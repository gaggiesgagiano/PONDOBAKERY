A production plan tells the bakers what to make on a shift, how many batches, and what to take from the store. Make one for every day shift and every night shift.

Make the plan before every shift

  1. Production → choose the date and Day shift or Night shift.
  2. Start with: all products that have a model stock for this shift (best), a copy of the last plan for this shift, or an empty plan → Make plan.
  3. For every product type what is still on hand on the shelf and how many are expired. The program shows the suggested number to make.
  4. Type the number to make (or keep the suggestion). Add other products with + Add product. The program works out the units and the number of batches.
  5. Set the status to Final – give to bakers and press Save plan.

Print for the bakers

  • Print plan – what to make, for the wall.
  • Print ingredient list – everything to take from the store, including what goes into the mother recipes. Tick each item as it leaves the store.
  • Print recipe cards – the recipes, scaled to the batches of this plan.

After the shift

  1. Open the plan and press Mark as done. The ingredients it used are taken off the stock.
Model stock = how many of a product you want on the shelf after the shift. Set it on the recipe (Edit recipe → Model stock day / night) and new plans fill themselves in.

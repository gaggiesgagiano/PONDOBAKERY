Everything that leaves the bakery without being sold is waste – and waste is money. Log it every shift, honestly. The target is at most 5% of sales.

Log the waste at the end of every shift

  1. Waste → choose the date and the shift → Start waste log. There is one log per shift.
  2. For every product: pick it from the list, type the quantity (kg for products sold per kg) and choose the reason: Expired, Damaged, Over-production, Burnt / quality, Customer return, Staff / tasting or Other. Add a note if it helps.
  3. Press Save waste log. A manager checks it and presses Sign off.
Staff meals and tastings are logged too, with the reason Staff / tasting – they are counted separately from real waste.

Monthly waste summary

Monthly waste summary shows the most wasted products, the waste per reason and day by day. Lots of Over-production? Lower the model stock of that product.

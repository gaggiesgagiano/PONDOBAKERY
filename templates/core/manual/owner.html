Only the Manager (the top login) sees Settings, Users & rights, E-mail settings and the change history, and can do everything in the program. Managers and supervisors can make backups if their rights allow it.

The people and their logins

  • Manager – first in charge. Everything, plus Settings and Users.
  • Assistant Manager – everything, also staff & payroll (not Settings or Users).
  • Supervisor and Admin – everything except staff & payroll.
  • Cashier – sales & cash-up, invoices and deliveries; can look at selling prices.
  • Baker – only recipes: to look at them and print recipe cards (no costs, no prices).

Letters are not part of any role – nobody except the Manager can write letters until you tick Letters for that person.

Add a person and choose their rights Manager

  1. Settings › Users & rights → Add person: username, name, password (at least 8 letters) and a starting role.
  2. The tick list opens, grouped like the menu: Freight & Logistics · Procurement & Inventory · Finance · Production · HR · Administration · Sales. Every part of the program has two ticks: Can see and Can change (“change” includes “see”). Add or remove single ticks, or use Tick everything / Untick everything.
  3. Office → Letters: no tick = Letters is hidden; Can see = open and reprint saved letters; Can change = write, change, print and delete letters. Only you can put your signature on a letter.
  4. Stock & orders covers Stock, Orders and GRV together.
  5. Two extra ticks: See costs & profit (ingredient prices, GP, dashboard figures, reports, GP check) and Make flash drive backups → Save rights.
  6. Forgotten password: type a new one next to the person → Set. Somebody left: Switch off – the record of what they did stays.

Staff cards

  • Settings › Staff cards: a photo card with a barcode for every staff member – scan to log in and to approve on the till. Choose each person's login next to their name. Make missing cards & print all → cut out and laminate. Lost card → New card.
  • The Money & accounts tick shows the owner's money – give it only to people you trust with the books.

Settings

  • Money settings: company name, VAT rate, target GP and “Show GP in red below %” (35%). Shop details (address, phone, VAT number, payment details) print on invoices, payslips and orders.
  • Costs & targets: the profit you want per month, trading days per week, the waste target and the monthly overheads. The dashboard works out break-even from this.
  • Categories & suppliers, Payment & expense types, Tax & NSSA rates (check the PAYE table with ZIMRA every year).
  • Change history: who changed what, and when – also every price change.
  • Look & feel: the colours for everybody and the log-in screen, and the tagline (“Easy to enjoy”). The brand font Pondo (Retro Star) is built in.

Pondo on other computers one main computer, many users

There is one Pondo, on the main computer. Everybody else opens it in Chrome on their own computer and logs in with their own username – nothing is installed on the other computers, and everybody sees the same, up-to-date figures. Up to about 10 people can work at the same time.

  1. Start Pondo on the main computer every morning and keep it switched on, plugged in and on the UPS / inverter during trading hours.
  2. Stop it going to sleep: Windows Settings › System › Power → “When plugged in, put my device to sleep after” = Never.
  3. The shop Wi-Fi must be set to Private (Settings › Network & internet › Wi-Fi › your network › Network profile type). The very first time Windows asks “Allow Python on networks?” – tick Private networks only and press Allow.
  4. The small Pondo window shows the address for the other computers, e.g. http://gaggies:8765 (or the number, e.g. http://192.168.0.106:8765). On each other computer: open Chrome, type the address, log in, make a bookmark.

Can't connect? Check that Pondo is running, the main computer is awake and on the same Wi-Fi, and try the number address instead of the name. The file CONNECTING OTHER COMPUTERS.txt in the Pondo folder has the same steps.

Pondo is only open on the bakery's private network – not on the internet. Working from home or an internet café is being set up separately; until then ask the Manager.

Change history and login register

  • Settings › Change history shows who changed what, and when – also every price change.
  • Settings › Login register (or the Logins tab on the same page) shows every log-in, log-out and wrong password: the date and time, the person, where (Main computer · Shop network · Outside) and the computer or phone (e.g. “Chrome on Android phone”).
  • Pick one person, or tick Only wrong passwords. Several wrong passwords in a row for one name can mean somebody is guessing – give that person a new password.

SOP exams owner only

Help & settings › SOP exams holds the 5 exam papers and their memos. Only the owner sees it, and it only opens after typing the login password again. It locks itself after 10 minutes (or press Lock now). Every opening is written in the change history. Staff who fail write again after one week on a different paper.

Phones

Phones on the shop Wi-Fi open Pondo the same way as other computers – see Getting started › Open the program on your phone. If a phone can't connect after a new laptop or a new router: double-click Allow phones and other computers in the Pondo folder (answer Yes) – it sets the shop Wi-Fi to Private and lets Pondo through the Windows firewall.

Daily backup every trading day, after the last cash-up

  1. The program makes a copy on the laptop by itself every time it starts – but if the laptop breaks or is stolen, only the flash drive copy helps.
  2. Every trading day, after the last cash-up: plug in the flash drive → Back up now → choose the drive. The data, the product photos and the staff files are copied to a “Pondo Backups” folder. Wait for “Backup saved”.
  3. After a cash-up is saved, Pondo pops up “Last cash-up of the day? … Back up now” as a reminder.
  4. Keep the flash drive locked away – not next to the laptop.
No backup – no start. If the last trading day's cash-ups were not backed up, Pondo is locked the next morning for everybody and shows “Make the backup first”. A Supervisor, the Assistant Manager or the Manager makes the backup, and Pondo opens again. Flash drive lost or broken? Only the Manager can open Pondo for that day without it – with a written reason that goes into the change history.

The password is kept scrambled on this laptop only. Also type the accountant's e-mail address. The list at the bottom shows every e-mail Pondo sent.

After that, invoices and quotations (✉ E-mail), supplier orders (✉ E-mail to supplier – see Orders) and the accountant report (✉ E-mail to accountant) can be e-mailed with the PDF attached. You can change the address, subject and message before you press Send e-mail; a copy goes to your own inbox.

Recipes & Prices lists every recipe in recipe-number order: what goes in, how many it makes, the method – and for the managers the cost, the selling price and the gross profit (GP).

Find a recipe

  • Type a name, a recipe number or an ingredient in the search box, or click a category. Mother recipes are doughs, fillings and creams that go into other recipes.
  • The line at the top shows how many numbers are used and free (201001–201999 = room for 999 recipes). Free numbers stay in the list as empty striped lines in their place.
  • The chips Below target and No price show the products that need attention.

On the recipe page

  • What goes in – in three parts: Dough / main, Topping / filling and Packaging.
  • The yield, the cost per unit, the selling price and the GP (managers only).
  • Print recipe card – one A4 page with the photo, ready for the recipe file (wide margin for the punch holes). Print scaled card for another quantity, Print costing for the office.
  • Photo: Add photo / Change photo – it is used on the recipe card, the price cards and the posters.

Make a new recipe managers

  1. + New recipe – or hover over an empty line in the list and click + new recipe to use that free number – or open a similar recipe and press Copy.
  2. The number box suggests the first free number; the free numbers under it can be clicked. Type the name, pick the category, and tick Mother recipe if it is used inside other recipes.
  3. Type the scaling weight per unit (kg of dough per item) and the weight loss in baking – or type the batch yield yourself.
  4. Under What goes in, start typing an ingredient and pick it from the list. Quantities are in the ingredient's unit: kg, litre or each. Packaging is usually “per finished item”.
  5. Type the selling price incl. VAT and choose Sold per: each, or kg for products sold by weight (put “p/kg” in the name). Leave “VAT on this product” empty for the normal rate; type 0 for zero-rated products like bread.
  6. Type the method (one step per line), baking temperature and time, allergens, and the model stock for day and night shift → Save recipe.
GP suddenly red? Check for a typing mistake – 5 kg instead of 0.005 kg – or an ingredient without a price.

Products you don't make any more: Archive this recipe instead of deleting it, so old reports stay right. An archived recipe keeps its number.

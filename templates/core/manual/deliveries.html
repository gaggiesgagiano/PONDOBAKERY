An order taken to a customer by a driver – usually one booked on inDrive. Deliveries are cash only: the customer pays the driver cash for the goods plus the delivery fee. The driver keeps the fee and brings back the money for the goods, which goes through the till.

Send an order with a driver every delivery

  1. Deliveries → + New delivery. (From a quotation or invoice: Send with a driver.)
  2. Customer & where to: pick the customer or type the name, cell, street, suburb and directions.
  3. Driver & car: pick the driver from the list – or type a new one from the inDrive app (name, cell, car registration, colour, make). A new driver goes on the Drivers list by himself.
  4. The products and the delivery fee the driver asked.
  5. Save & make the invoice → Print delivery note (one copy goes with the driver, one stays at the till) and Print invoice for the customer.

When the driver comes back same day

  1. Count the cash. The program shows what the driver must bring back: the goods – he keeps the fee.
  2. Ring the goods up on the till.
  3. Open the delivery → type the money, the amount on the till and the till slip number → Check off. Short or over shows in red; the invoice is marked paid.

Drivers & ID cards

Deliveries → Drivers: open a driver → Take photo of him and of the car (with the number plate), add his ID number → Print ID card. Cut it out, fold it in the middle and laminate it. The list shows how often each driver brought back too little.

Never let an order leave before the car and number plate are checked against the driver's card or the inDrive app. If the order comes back, press Cancel – the order came back.

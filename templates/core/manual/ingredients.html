The ingredient list holds every bought ingredient, packaging item and mother recipe, with its pack size and price. All recipe costs come from here.

Add or change an ingredient

  1. Ingredients → + New ingredient. To change one: press Edit at the top of the list and click the ingredient – or open it and press Change price / edit.
  2. Type the name, the kind (bought, packaging or mother recipe), the supplier, the unit (kg, litre, each…), the pack size and the pack price – and how much VAT is included in that price.
  3. Under Stock levels type Re-order when stock is below and Order up to – the program then suggests orders.
  4. Save. Every recipe that uses it gets the new cost straight away; the old price is kept in the price history.
An ingredient without a price counts as $0 and makes products look cheaper than they are. Fix it straight away.
When a supplier's price changes, the GRV can update the price for you (see Stock). Then check GP check (or Recipes & Prices) for products whose GP turned orange or burgundy.

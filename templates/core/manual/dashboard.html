The dashboard is the first page after you log in. At the top are your Short Cuts – one tile for every job you do often. Below them the managers see how the bakery is doing.

Short Cuts

Every Short Cut is a tile in the colour of its area, in the same order as the menu on the left. Click a tile to go straight to that job.

  • A solid coloured tile with “To do” still has to be done today.
  • A white tile with “✓ Done” is finished for today.
  • “Due”, “Check”, “Overdue” or “Order” on a tile means it needs attention.

The standard Short Cuts

  • Type the day's sales – done when the day's sales from the till are typed.
  • Cash-up – done when a till is cashed up; also shows how many cash-ups still need a manager's sign-off.
  • Deliveries – orders out with drivers and the money still to come back.
  • Production plan – done when there is a plan for the day (day and night shift).
  • Waste log – done when yesterday's waste is written down.
  • Change prices – opens Recipes & Prices ready to type new selling prices.
  • Price cards – print shelf cards and posters.
  • Receiving – type goods in from suppliers (GRV).
  • Payroll – this month's pay run: not started, busy, or ✓ final.
  • Add staff – opens the page for a new staff member.
  • Staff papers – only shows when a passport, permit or medical certificate has run out or runs out soon.
  • Accountant report – in the first 10 days of the month: last month's report, to send. Later: this month so far.
  • Back up – shows (“Due”) when today's cash-ups are not on the flash drive yet. The backup is made every day after the last cash-up; without it Pondo is locked the next morning.

You only see Short Cuts for the parts of the program your login may use.

Choose and arrange your Short Cuts

  1. Change the order: drag a tile to where you want it – with a mouse anywhere on the tile, on a touch screen with the six dots at the bottom right. “Order saved” shows at the bottom. Everybody keeps their own order.
  2. Choose short cuts: tick the ones you want. Extra ones you can switch on: Unpaid invoices, Open quotations, Low stock, Stock take, Orders on the way, Equipment count and Month-end report.
  3. + Add my own job – e.g. “Clean the display fridges”, on the days you choose (a manager can add one for everybody). Press Tick done on it when it is finished – it starts again the next day.
  4. To go back to the standard list and order, press Back to the standard list on the Choose page.

For the managers

  • The coloured status band says if the month is on target: teal = above break-even plus profit, warm orange = above break-even only, burgundy = below break-even. The black line on the bar is break-even.
  • The four tiles: sales of the day, the week, the month, and the profit of the month so far.
  • Our sales compares the day, week and month with break-even (the sales that just pay all the costs) and break-even plus the profit you want.
  • Rest of the month tells you how much you still have to sell – and how much per day for the days that are left.
  • Sales per category, Waste (of the day before), What we need to sell, Per staff member, the Till and Products to check (products with a GP below 35% – they show in solid red).
  • Use ← → or the date box to look at another day. Print (1 page) prints it on one A4 page.

Pondo is an everyday bakery: good food that feels like a good deal without feeling ordinary – easy to enjoy.

Mission

To make quality bakery treats accessible, convenient and enjoyable every day.

Vision

To become Zimbabwe's go-to everyday bakery for quality, value and joy.

Our values

  • Quality – consistently fresh, satisfying and well crafted.
  • Simplicity – easy to choose, easy to grab, easy to enjoy.
  • Accessibility – good bakery food that doesn't feel expensive or out of reach.
  • Consistency – the same quality and experience, every time.
  • Joy – small moments that make people smile.
  • Community – familiar, welcoming and part of daily life.

How we talk to customers

Friendly, upbeat, conversational and playful – a bakery that knows its craft but doesn't take itself too seriously. Greet every customer, smile, and keep it simple. “Pondo yako chete!” On social media: warm, close-up photos of fresh food and happy people, and never the same background colour twice in a row.

Logos – do's & don'ts

  • Use the logo files from Our Brand, exactly as they are.
  • Only the five brand colours, in the pairings above.
  • Keep clear space around the logo.
  • Small space? Use the round badge or the Pondo character.
  • Don't skew or stretch the logo.
  • Don't change its colours.
  • Don't change the layout.
  • Don't add a stroke or a drop shadow.
  • Don't tweak the letters.
  • Don't put graphics into or behind it.

The till every sale · practice mode until ZIMRA is connected

  1. Open the till: menu Till → choose Till 1 or Till 2, type the float you counted with the manager ($200) and the day's ZiG rate → Open the till.
  2. Sell: tap the products (or scan / type the PLU number and press Enter). Use − / + for more of the same. Products sold per kg ask for the weight.
  3. Pay: press PAY, choose Cash, Swipe / card, EcoCash or ZiG cash, type or tap the amount ($ notes, Exact) and press Add this payment. Split payments are allowed. EcoCash needs the reference from the confirmation message. Press Complete sale – the slip prints and the change shows big on the screen.
  4. Reprint or refund: Sales & refunds at the top. A refund needs the Supervisor's (up to $5) or a manager's (more) own username and password – a cashier can never approve their own refund. Log the product as waste (Customer return).
  5. Cash drop (button at the top): when the drawer has more than $500 the till shows a red warning. The manager counts the drop with the cashier and types their own username and password – only managers have the safe key. A drop slip prints for the cash bag.
  6. Pay-out (gas, transport, small purchases): Supervisor or manager approves with their own password; choose what it was for and the receipt number. It goes on the cash-up and in the expenses by itself.
  7. No sale: opening the drawer without a sale needs the Supervisor's password and a reason – every opening is recorded.
  8. Refunds: tick Product goes to waste (on by default) and Pondo logs it as waste (Customer return); untick when the product goes back on the shelf.
  9. Close the till at the end of the shift: count the drawer with the Supervisor (float included, ZiG separately) and type the amounts. Pondo makes the cash-up (over / short), the product sales and the daily sales by itself – nothing to type over from a till slip. The Supervisor then signs the cash-up off as usual.
  10. Next shift: the till is free again. The next cashier re-opens it with a fresh float from the safe – every shift has its own cash-up (e.g. Till 1 and Till 1 #2).
Staff cards (barcodes): the Manager makes and prints a photo card for every staff member under Settings → Staff cards (Make missing cards & print all) and chooses each person's Pondo login next to their name. The card shows the photo, name, job, staff no. and the barcode – one side: cut out and laminate. On the login screen click Or scan your staff card and scan – no typing. On a refund, cash drop, pay-out or no sale the Supervisor or manager scans their own card instead of typing the password. Changing cashier at the till: press Log out at the top right and the next cashier scans their card. A lost card: press New card – the old one stops working at once.
Until Pondo is connected to ZIMRA's fiscal system every slip says PRACTICE – NOT A TAX INVOICE. Receipt printer: set it as the default printer; to print without the print window, start Chrome with --kiosk-printing (ask the admin).

Every day after closing the counter types the sales and cashes up every till. The tabs are Daily sales · Cash-ups · Expenses · Product sales · Over & short per cashier · Month summary · Payment & expense types (the last three for managers).

1 · Type the day's sales every day after closing

  1. Print the till's end-of-day (Z) report.
  2. Go to Sales & Cash-Up → Daily sales and press Type today's sales (or click the “Type the day's sales” job on the dashboard).
  3. Type the sales of every category from the report excluding VAT. Day and night shift go together on one day.
  4. Press Save & next day → or Save & see the dashboard.
Made a mistake? Open the day again, change the number and save. To remove a day completely, empty all the boxes and save.

2 · Cash up every till every day after closing

Pondo has two tills: Till 1 and Till 2. Every till gets its own cash-up every day – on a busy day with both tills open you do two cash-ups. Each cash-up carries the name of the cashier who worked that till, so over and short can be added up per person.

  1. Cash-ups tab → under Cash up a till choose the date, the till (Till 1 or Till 2) and the cashier – pick the name from the staff list → Start cash-up.
  2. Step 1 – What the till says: type the till's total for every payment type (Cash US$, EcoCash, Swipe / card, Bank transfer …). For non-cash payments also type what was really received (EcoCash statement, card machine slip).
  3. Step 2 – Money paid out of the till: press + Add pay-out for every payment made from the drawer – what it was for, who got it, the amount and the slip number.
  4. Step 3 – Count the cash: type the float at the start, all the cash counted in the drawer (notes and coins, float included), ZiG notes and the ZiG rate of the day if customers paid in ZiG, the float left for tomorrow and the cash taken to the bank or safe.
  5. Press Save cash-up. The program shows if the till is over or short. Print gives a slip for the cash bag.
  6. The manager checks the money and presses Sign off. A signed-off cash-up can only be changed after Remove sign-off.
  7. Pondo then asks “Last cash-up of the day?”. When all tills are cashed up, the Supervisor or Manager presses Back up now and makes the daily backup on the flash drive. Cashiers: tell the Supervisor. No backup – no start: without it Pondo is locked the next morning.
Short or over? Count the drawer again before you save, and write what happened in the notes. Never “fix” the numbers. Deliveries rung up on the till show on the cash-up under Deliveries on the till.

Wrong cash-up? In the list press Edit and click the cash-up, or open it and press Edit. A cash-up typed twice can be removed with Delete cash-up at the bottom.

Over & short per cashier managers · daily, weekly, monthly

  1. Sales & Cash-Up → the tab Over & short per cashier.
  2. Choose Day, Week (Monday to Sunday) or Month, pick a date and press Show. ‹ Previous and Next › move one day, week or month.
  3. The Summary shows every cashier with the number of cash-ups, their sales, the total over, the total short and the net – biggest short first. “Not signed off” shows cash-ups the manager still has to check. Under it: the totals per till.
  4. Below the summary, every cashier has their own table with each cash-up: date, till, sales and over / short.
  5. Print prints it on A4 with lines for the cashier's and the manager's signature – keep it in the cash-up file or discuss it with the cashier.
The report adds up by the cashier's name, so always pick the name from the list on the cash-up – never type it differently.

3 · Expenses

Money paid for running the bakery (gas, airtime, cleaning, transport …): + Expense → date, type, amount, VAT on the receipt (only if Pondo is VAT registered and the receipt shows VAT), paid from, paid to, what for and the receipt number. Pay-outs from the till are added automatically.

Stock bought from suppliers is not an expense – it goes on a GRV (GRV – goods received). Wages come from the payroll.

4 · Product sales optional

To see best sellers and the GP of the day, type how many of each product were sold (from the till's product report): Product sales → pick the day → start with an empty list, yesterday's products or all products → type the quantities → Save product sales. Products sold per kg are typed in kg (e.g. 2.5). Fill in from product sales on the daily sales page then fills in the categories for you.

5 · Month summary

Sales day by day, how customers paid, the expenses and the best sellers of the month. Use ← → for other months and Print.

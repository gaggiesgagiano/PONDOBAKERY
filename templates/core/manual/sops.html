The SOP file holds the standard operating procedures: how we do things, the same way every time – opening and closing, cash, production, food safety, stock, customers, staff rules and emergencies. Every staff member must read and sign the SOPs for their job.

Read an SOP everybody

  1. SOP file (menu, Help & settings). The SOPs are grouped in 8 sections and numbered: SOP 2.05 = section 2, number 5.
  2. Click an SOP to read it: the purpose, who is responsible (job titles), who must read it, and the procedure step by step.
  3. Search for a word – e.g. safe, refund, cleaning.

Write or change an SOP Manager

  1. + New SOP, or Edit at the top of the list and click the SOP.
  2. Section and number, title, purpose, responsible (job titles – never names, so the file also works for other shops), and tick who must read it.
  3. Type the procedure. Buttons: B I U, Heading, Normal, 1. Steps for numbered steps and • Points for bullet points. Enter = next step, Enter twice = end the list.
  4. Save. A new SOP is a Draft.

Approve Owner / Director

Read the draft with the Owner / Director. Under Approval type who approved it and the date → Approve version 1. It must be reviewed again within a year – the list shows “Review due”.

Changing an approved SOP makes a new version: the old one is kept under Earlier versions, the new one is a draft until it is approved again, and everybody must sign again. Type What changed when you save.

Staff sign that they read it

  1. Open the SOP → Print sign-off sheet. It lists everybody who must read it, with space to sign.
  2. Explain the SOP to the team and let everybody sign the sheet.
  3. In Pondo tick the people who signed, choose the date → Save – these people signed. Keep the paper sheet in the SOP file.
  4. Who still has to read shows, per staff member, which SOPs they must still sign – orange ones are SOPs that changed since they signed.

Print

Print the whole file – a cover, the contents and every SOP on its own A4 page, with the version, approval and review dates and signature lines. Drafts print with “DRAFT” across the page. Print SOP on an SOP prints only that one – e.g. to put up on the wall where the job is done.

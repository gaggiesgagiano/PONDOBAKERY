The equipment register lists every oven, mixer, tray and fridge with its asset number, what it cost, and what it is worth after depreciation. The tabs are Register · Monthly count · Depreciation · Categories & places.

Add equipment

  1. + New item → asset number (write it on the item too, e.g. EQ-144), name, category, place, serial number, supplier, purchase date, cost each excl. VAT and how many.
  2. Save. Depreciation is worked out every month by itself.
  3. Sold, scrapped or broken? Open the item and fill in Gone? – the date and what happened.

Monthly count month end

  1. Monthly count → Print count sheet (or take the laptop).
  2. Walk around, count every item and type the number. Fewer than bought turns red.
  3. Save count and tell the Manager what is missing.

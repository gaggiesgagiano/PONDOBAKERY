Pondo Bakery has its own program – Pondo – for sales, cash-ups, recipes, prices, stock, orders, staff, letters and reports. It runs on one main computer in the bakery; everybody else opens it from their own computer with their own login. This manual shows you, step by step, how to do your part.

Open the program on the main computer

  1. Double-click the Pondo Bakery icon (the smiling Pondo character) on the desktop or the taskbar. A small Pondo window opens and, after a few seconds, the program opens in Chrome.
  2. The small Pondo window must stay open while people work – closing it stops Pondo for everybody. It also shows the address the other computers use, e.g. http://gaggies:8765.
  3. Type your own username and password and press Log in.
  4. When you are finished, press Log out at the bottom of the menu.

Open the program on another computer in the shop

  1. Make sure the main computer is on, awake and Pondo is running (small Pondo window open).
  2. On the other computer open Chrome and type the address from the small Pondo window, e.g. http://gaggies:8765. If the name doesn't work, use the number it shows, e.g. http://192.168.0.106:8765.
  3. Log in with your own username and password. Make a bookmark so it is one click next time.

Open the program on your phone inside the shop

  1. Connect the phone to the shop Wi-Fi (not mobile data). The main computer must be on with Pondo running.
  2. Open Chrome and type the address with http:// in front, e.g. http://192.168.0.106:8765 – the number is in the small Pondo window.
  3. Log in with your own username and password.
  4. Make a shortcut: Chrome's ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen (sometimes under Share). Pondo then opens with one tap.
  • On a phone the menu is folded away: tap ☰ Menu at the top to open it, ✕ Close to close it. The bar also shows the name of the page you are on; the Pondo character takes you to the Dashboard.
  • Lists that are wider than the phone show “Swipe for more →” – swipe the list sideways to see the other columns.
  • From outside the shop (home, mobile data) Pondo can't be opened yet – that is still being set up.
Never use somebody else's login and never tell anybody your password. Pondo writes down who changed what and when.

Find your way – the menu on the left

The menu is grouped. You only see the parts your login is allowed to use.

  • Dashboard is always at the top.
  • Freight & Logistics: Deliveries.
  • Procurement: Orders · GRV – goods received · Ingredients.
  • Inventory: Stock · Waste · Equipment.
  • Finance: Money & accounts · Reports · GP check · Excel downloads.
  • Production: Production · Recipes & Prices.
  • Tax & Compliance: Fiscal (ZIMRA).
  • HR: Staff & Payroll (staff records, advances, leave, pay runs and payslips) · SOP file · SOP exams.
  • Administration: Letters · Forms · Settings · Back up now · Staff manual · Quick help · Our Brand.
  • Sales: Till · Sales & Cash-Up · Invoices & Quotes.
  • Every area has its own brand colour – the coloured band at the top of a page and the little coloured square next to the menu item. Sales is teal, Production warm orange, Waste burgundy, Orders orange, and so on. The Short Cuts on the dashboard use the same colours.
  • The teal buttons under the band (for example Daily sales · Cash-ups · Expenses) take you to the other pages of that area. The page you are on is burgundy.
  • Everything you can type in or tick is teal – boxes, lists, dates and tick boxes. Text that is not teal can't be changed there.
  • In long lists the column headings stay at the top while you scroll. Click anywhere on a line to open it.
  • The round ? Help button at the bottom right of every page opens this manual at the right chapter.

Typing in boxes

  • When you click or Tab into a number box, the whole number is selected – just type the new number over it. No need to delete first.
  • Press Tab to go to the next box, Shift + Tab to go back.
  • Nothing is kept until you press the Save button. Leaving the page without saving loses what you typed.
  • A box with a red border and a message under it was not accepted – fix it and save again.

Changing something in a list – the Edit button

Lists such as Ingredients, Recipes, Orders, GRVs, Cash-ups, Product sales, Invoices, Deliveries, Drivers, Production plans, Waste logs, Staff, Pay runs and Letters have an Edit button at the top.

  1. Press Edit. It turns orange and says Choose a line…, and a bar at the bottom says Click the line you want to edit.
  2. Click the line you want to change – it opens straight in edit mode.
  3. Changed your mind? Press Cancel in the bar or the Esc key.

Make it yours

  • My look (bottom of the menu): pick your colours – Pondo Classic, Orange, Teal, Warm Orange, Bakery Cream or the dark Late Shift – and Large text if the letters are too small. Only you see your choice.
  • Change password (bottom of the menu). Choose one nobody can guess – at least 8 letters.

Printing

Most pages have a Print button. In the print window choose your printer – or Save as PDF to send it on WhatsApp or e-mail. Everything prints on A4; the dashboard and the month-end report on one A4 page. Teal boxes print as plain text. If the date, page address or “1/1” print at the top or bottom of the page, untick Headers and footers under More settings in the print window.

Lists also have an Excel button, and Excel downloads in the menu has them all together.

Golden rules
  • Money is in US$. Sales, costs and stock values are excluding VAT; selling prices and the till include VAT.
  • Type things on the same day – sales, cash-up, waste and goods received – while you still remember.
  • If something looks wrong, don't guess: tell the manager.

Break-even
The sales (excl. VAT) that just pay all the costs of the bakery – wages and overheads. Below it the bakery loses money.
Break-even + profit
The sales needed to pay all costs and make the profit the bakery wants.
GP (gross profit)
Selling price excl. VAT minus the cost of the ingredients and packaging. As a % of the selling price; the target is in Settings.
Excl. / incl. VAT
Without / with VAT. Sales and costs in reports are excl. VAT; selling prices and the till are incl. VAT.
Z-report
The till's end-of-day report with the day's totals.
Float
The small change left in the till to start the next day.
Cash-up
Counting the money in a till and checking it against what the till says.
Over / short
More / less money in the drawer than the till says.
Sign off
A manager checked and approved a cash-up or waste log.
Pay-out
Money paid out of the till drawer, e.g. for gas or transport.
ZiG
Zimbabwe Gold notes. Counted separately and changed into US$ at the rate of the day.
GRV
Goods received voucher – what really arrived from a supplier, at the invoice prices.
Model stock
How many of a product you want on the shelf after a shift. Production plans start from it.
Mother recipe
A dough, filling or cream made in the bakery and used in other recipes.
Scaling weight
The weight of dough per item, before baking.
Batch yield
How many items one batch of a recipe makes.
Sold per kg
A product sold by weight; its price, counts, sales and waste are in kg.
Stock take
Counting everything in the store and on the shop floor to find the real stock value.
Unexplained loss
Stock that is gone but was not sold or logged as waste.
Depreciation
The part of the cost of equipment that is “used up” every month. Book value = cost minus depreciation so far.
Pro-forma invoice
An invoice sent before the goods, so the customer can pay first.
Pay run
The payroll of one month for everybody.
NSSA · PAYE · AIDS levy · ZIMDEF
Social security, income tax, the 3% levy on PAYE and the training levy the business pays.
Staff number
Each person's own number: the year they started and a running number, like 26007. It is on payslips, the fingerprint machine, the staff card and the file sheet, and is never given to somebody else.
Soft copy / hard copy
Soft copy = the scan or photo of a paper, saved on the laptop. Hard copy = the paper itself, in the person's staff file.
Short Cuts
The tiles at the top of the dashboard – one for every job you do often, in the order of the menu.
Till 1 · Till 2
The bakery's two tills. Each one is cashed up on its own, with the name of its cashier.
Over & short per cashier
The report that adds up every cashier's over and short for a day, a week or a month.
PO (purchase order)
An order to a supplier, numbered PO-1001, PO-1002 … Sent by e-mail or WhatsApp from Pondo.
Letterhead
Pondo's printed heading with the logo and contact details. Letters print page 1 on it; LT-0001 is a letter number.
Edit button
The button at the top of a list: press it, then click the line you want to change.
Main computer
The laptop Pondo runs on. Other computers open Pondo in Chrome through the shop network.
Login register
The list of every log-in, log-out and wrong password – who, when and from which computer or phone (Settings).

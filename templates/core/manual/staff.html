Staff records with photos and personal papers, advances, leave and the monthly payroll with NSSA, PAYE, AIDS levy and ZIMDEF. The tabs are Staff · Staff files · Payroll · Tax & NSSA rates.

Add a staff member

  1. + New staff member (or the Add staff Short Cut on the dashboard) → personal details, job and department, the start date. Leave Staff no. empty – the program gives the next number for the year they started (e.g. 26024). Use the same number on the fingerprint machine.
  2. Pay: pay type (monthly, daily or hourly), the salary or rate, transport and other allowances, deduct NSSA / PAYE, NSSA number, tax number, how they are paid and the bank or EcoCash details, leave days a year.
  3. When someone leaves: type the date they left and untick Still working here.

To change somebody's details: in the staff list press Edit and click the person – or open them and press Edit.

Photo, papers and the staff file

  1. On the person's page, Photo → Add photo (from a phone) or Take photo → Snap (laptop camera).
  2. Staff file → + Add papers → choose the scan or photo (ID front and back can go together), what paper it is, the date it runs out (passport, permit, medical certificate) → Save papers.
  3. When the paper copy goes into the file, tick In the file next to that paper.
  4. Print file sheet and put it in front of the paper file. Tick the boxes by hand as papers go in.

The Staff files tab shows whose file is not complete, which papers run out within 30 days, and how staff numbers look (26001 – never given twice). Print staff cards prints bank-card size cards with photo and number, 8 per page.

On a person's page you can record an advance (Save advance) and leave (Save leave). Advances are taken off the next pay run.

The monthly pay run month end

  1. Staff & Payroll → Payroll (or the Payroll Short Cut on the dashboard) → under Start a pay run choose the month and the pay date → Start pay run.
  2. The pay run page shows one card per person, with four steps at the top: Fill in each person · Save & work out · Check the net pay · Make final. At the top right of each card is their take-home pay and the pay before tax.
  3. Monthly staff get their full salary – only type something if they had unpaid days (empty = full month). Day staff need days worked, hourly staff need hours worked – that box has a burgundy border. Every card also has Overtime hours and Sunday / holiday hours.
  4. Allowances, bonus, other pay, advances and other deductions are under + Extra pay & deductions on the card – click it to open. It opens by itself when something is filled in.
  5. The dark bar at the bottom stays on screen while you scroll: the totals of the pay run and the buttons Save & work out and Save and close. Press Save & work out often – the program works out NSSA, PAYE, AIDS levy and the net pay. A card with a mistake gets a red border and a red message.
  6. Somebody missing? Tick them under Add people to this pay run at the bottom and save. Someone who must not be paid this month: tick Leave … off this pay run at the bottom of their card.
  7. When every card is right: open the pay run → Make final → Print payslips (two per page) and Print summary. Excel downloads it. Re-open if something must still change.
Only people with staff rights can open personal papers; the flash drive backup has a copy too – lock it away. Check the rates under Tax & NSSA rates whenever ZIMRA or NSSA announce new tables.

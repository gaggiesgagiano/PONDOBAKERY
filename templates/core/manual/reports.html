The reports show how the month really went. The tabs are Month-end · Stock results · Month archive.

Month-end report first days of the new month

  • 1 · Profit & loss – sales, cost of sales, gross profit, wages, running expenses, depreciation and the net profit against the profit you want.
  • 2 · Expenses by type against the budget, 3 · Cash & bank, 4 · VAT for the accountant, 5 · Sales & stock, 6 · Purchases by supplier and 7 · Equipment.
  • Print (1 page) for the directors and the accountant.
  1. Make sure everything of the month is typed: daily sales, cash-ups, expenses, GRVs, the final stock take, the pay run and the equipment count.
  2. Press Lock this month. The figures go into the Month archive and don't change any more.
  3. In the archive, write the date you sent it to the accountant.

Stock results

The real GP between two stock takes (opening stock + purchases − closing stock) next to the GP the recipes say you should make. The difference after waste is the unexplained loss – theft, over-portioning or counting mistakes.

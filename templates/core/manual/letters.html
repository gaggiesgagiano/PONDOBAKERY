Letters is where you type a business letter, print it on the Pondo letterhead, keep it, and print it again later. Forms has the empty Pondo forms and the blank letterhead to print. Letter numbers go LT-0001, LT-0002 …

Who may use Letters

Not everybody may write letters. The Manager gives the right per person under Settings › Users & rights, heading Office: No access (Letters is not in their menu), Can look (open and reprint saved letters only) or Can look & change (write, change, print and delete). Only the Manager can add Jurie's signature.

Write a letter

  1. Letters (menu, Help & settings) → + New letter.
  2. Date (today is filled in), Subject (prints as “Re: …”) and To – the name, company and address, one per line.
  3. Type the letter in the big Letter box. Start with the greeting (“Dear Mr Moyo”) and end with the closing (“Yours sincerely”). Press Enter for a new paragraph.
  4. Signed by and Job title: for the Manager it is always Jurie Gagiano, Manager; for others their own name.
  5. Add Jurie's signature (Manager only): the scanned signature prints above the name. Untick it to sign by hand.
  6. Save & print – the letter is saved and the print page opens. Or Save to print later.

Bold, italics and underline

  • Select the words, then press Ctrl + B for bold, Ctrl + I for italics, Ctrl + U to underline – or use the B I U buttons above the letter. Press the same keys again to switch it off.
  • The letter is in Arial Nova (plain Arial on a computer without Arial Nova), 11 point.
  • Text pasted from somewhere else comes in as plain words – its fonts and colours are left out.
  • Spelling mistakes get a red line while you type; right-click a word for suggestions.

How the letter prints

  • Page 1 is on the Pondo letterhead: the logo and the contact details at the top, the brand band at the bottom.
  • A long letter carries on onto plain pages by itself – page 2, 3 … have no letterhead.
  • The date, “Our ref: LT-0001”, the address, “Re: subject”, the letter, the signature, name, title and “Pondo Bakery” are placed for you.
  • In the print window choose A4. If a date or web address prints at the top or bottom, untick Headers and footers under More settings.

Find, reprint, change or delete a letter

  1. Letters shows every saved letter, newest first. Type in the search box (a name, subject or word in the letter) → Search.
  2. Reprint: press Print on the line – or click the letter to see it first.
  3. Change: press Edit at the top and click the letter, or open it and press Edit. Change it and save – it keeps its number.
  4. Delete: open it in Edit and press Delete this letter at the bottom, then OK. A deleted letter can't be brought back.

Blank letterhead and forms

  • Blank letterhead on the Letters page – or Forms → Office → Letterhead – prints an empty letterhead to write on by hand.
  • Forms (menu) has the empty Pondo forms to print and fill in by hand, on A4 with the shop details from Settings:
    • Office: letterhead.
    • Food safety & cleaning: shift cleaning checklist (one per shift per week), Saturday deep-cleaning checklist (13:00 – 16:00), fridge & freezer temperature log (one per month), pest control record. File them in the food safety file.
    • Health & safety: fire extinguisher & first aid kit record – file it in the health & safety file.
    • Customers: customer order form, complaints register (kept in a file at the counter, checked daily).
    • Staff: leave application, staff information, staff assessment, fixed term contract, uniform issue & return (kept in the staff file).
    • Hiring: staff application.

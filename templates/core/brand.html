Our Brand · the Pondo Bakery brand guidelines

Pondo Bakery

Pondo is an everyday bakery built around a simple idea: good food should feel like a good deal without feeling ordinary.

We make freshly prepared bakery treats for busy Zimbabweans who want something genuinely satisfying without spending too much time or money – proper bakery expertise in an easy, grab-and-go format.

Mission

Pondo exists to make quality bakery food an accessible part of everyday life. We create freshly made treats that balance quality, value and convenience, giving busy Zimbabweans something genuinely satisfying without unnecessary fuss. We want every visit to feel simple and enjoyable — good food, fair value and a little moment of pleasure in the day.

To make quality bakery treats accessible, convenient and enjoyable every day.

Vision

Pondo aims to become a recognisable and trusted everyday bakery brand in Zimbabwe, changing how people think about affordable bakery food. We want to prove that accessible doesn't have to mean generic or compromised, building a bakery experience people know, trust and enjoy.

To become Zimbabwe's go-to everyday bakery for quality, value and joy.

Purpose

Pondo exists to make the everyday treat feel like a small win. We bridge the gap between affordable bakery food and quality craftsmanship, making something genuinely good easier to enjoy.

  1. 01. Make quality accessible
  2. 02. Make everyday moments better
  3. 03. Make good food easy

Our values

They guide how we work, how we talk and how we show up for our customers.

Quality

We take pride in making bakery products that are consistently fresh, satisfying and well crafted.

Simplicity

We keep the experience straightforward — easy to choose, easy to grab and easy to enjoy.

Accessibility

We believe good bakery food should be enjoyable without feeling expensive, exclusive or out of reach.

Consistency

We strive to give customers the same quality and experience they can rely on every time they choose Pondo.

Joy

We bring personality and positivity to the everyday, creating small moments that make people smile.

Community

We are an everyday bakery made for the people around us, creating something that feels familiar, welcoming and part of daily life.

Brand voice & personality

Pondo feels friendly, playful and confident – a bakery that knows its craft but doesn't take itself too seriously. We talk the way people talk: conversational, upbeat and natural, adding personality without sounding forced or overly formal. Use this for signs, WhatsApp messages, social media and how staff greet customers.

Do's & don'ts

✓ Use the logo files from this page – exactly as they are.
✓ Only use the five brand colours, in the pairings above.
✓ Keep clear space around the logo.
✗ Don't skew or stretch the logo.
✗ Don't change it to colours outside the brand.
✗ Don't change the layout or arrangement.
✗ Don't add a stroke or a drop shadow.
✗ Don't tweak individual letters.
✗ Don't put graphics into or behind the logo.

Social media & photos

  • Checkerboard: never post the same background colour twice in a row – alternate brand colours so every post stands out.
  • Use the brand fonts only: the Pondo font for big words, Nunito for the rest. No random fonts.
  • Stick to the five brand colours so people recognise Pondo straight away – highlights, stories and posts.
  • Photos: warm, close-up, freshly baked food and real people enjoying it. Add a sticker (starburst “Today's special”, “Only $1.80”) or the round “Fresh since ’26” badge.
  • Make your own price cards and posters in this program: Products & Prices › Price cards & posters.

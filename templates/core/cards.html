Staff cards

A photo card with a barcode for every staff member. Scan it to log in, and Supervisors / managers scan it to approve refunds, pay-outs, cash drops and no-sales on the till. Later it opens the staff account too. The card holds a secret code – never the password.

Print all cards
Treat a card like a key: a manager's card can approve refunds and cash drops. Lost or copied (even a photo of the barcode)? Press New card – the old one stops working at once. When somebody leaves (untick Still working here) their card stops working by itself.

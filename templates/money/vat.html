Good to know

Input VAT can only be claimed with a proper tax invoice showing the supplier's VAT number – keep every supplier invoice with its GRV.

GRV VAT is worked out from each ingredient's VAT rate (Ingredients). Basic foods can be zero-rated or exempt – your accountant confirms the rates and the period (monthly or every 2 months) and files the return.

Delivery invoices are left out of the invoice line – their money is rung up on the till and is already in the till VAT.

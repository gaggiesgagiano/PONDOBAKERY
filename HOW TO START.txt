PONDO BAKERY PROGRAM – HOW TO START
===================================

NOTHING TO INSTALL. Python (the engine the program runs on) is inside the "python" folder.

ONE TIME ONLY
1. Put this whole "Pondo Bakery" folder on the laptop (e.g. C:\Pondo Bakery).
   Keep everything in it together – Pondo Bakery.exe needs the folders next to it.
2. Pin it to the taskbar: right-click "Pondo Bakery.exe" > "Show more options" (Windows 11) > "Pin to taskbar".
3. Want an icon on the desktop and in the Start menu too? Double-click "Create desktop icon".
   (Windows always shows a small arrow on desktop icons of programs – that's normal.)

EVERY DAY
1. Click the Pondo Bakery (donut) icon on the taskbar.
2. A small Pondo window appears, and after a few seconds the program opens in your browser.
   KEEP THE SMALL PONDO WINDOW OPEN while you work (you can minimise it).
   Closed the browser by mistake? Click "Open Pondo" in the small window.
3. When you are finished for the day: click "Close Pondo" in the small window.

MOVING TO ANOTHER LAPTOP
- Close Pondo first, then copy the WHOLE "Pondo Bakery" folder (flash drive is fine).
- Your information is in the "data" folder, so it moves with it.
- Use the program on ONE laptop only. Two copies do not share information.

THE VERY FIRST TIME the program opens, it asks you to choose your owner username and password.
Your recipes, ingredients and prices from the Excel master file are already inside.

BACKUPS
- The program keeps an automatic copy on the laptop every time it starts (data\backups),
  made BEFORE any update, so an update can always be undone.
- Once a week: plug in your flash drive and click "Back up now" in the menu.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- Pondo shows a message: take a photo of it and send it to Claude.
- For more detail, double-click "Start Pondo.bat". It starts the program with a black window
  that shows any error. Take a photo of the black window and send it to Claude.
